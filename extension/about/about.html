avif

avif - encode an image to AVIF on-device. Use to convert a PNG/JPEG/WebP to the smaller AVIF format. In/out: base64 image text on stdin to AVIF bytes on stdout (base64 at the boundary). Flags: --quality <1..100> (default 80); --speed <1..10> (default 10).
